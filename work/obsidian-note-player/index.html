Note Player

Your notes are your playlist.

Kind Obsidian plugins Language TypeScript License MIT ★ 2 stars updated Sep 2026

Turn any note into a music playlist. Add YouTube links or local files with Obsidian's embed syntax, play them as audio inside the vault, and keep the music library where the knowledge lives.

Playlists are just Markdown notes. Add URLs, organize them with your own metadata, and play them without leaving Obsidian. The player is audio-only on purpose.

Audio is downloaded and cached locally with yt-dlp, so playback is instant and works offline. The current queue survives between sessions, and companion Bases views organize the library.

Installrelease

brew install yt-dlp
# Download main.js, manifest.json, styles.css from the latest release into
.obsidian/plugins/obsidian-note-player/
