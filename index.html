    03

    Operating principles

    How we work

    XIA is a small lab: one operator, a vault, and a growing set of agents. These rules keep it calm.

    • Calm over clutter.Fewer surfaces, clearer ownership, less background noise.
    • Evidence before status.A task is complete when the result can be inspected, not when it is announced.
    • Durable over disposable.Decisions, context, and tools should survive the current session.
    • Automation with an owner.One operator, explicit boundaries, reversible changes.

    05

    Why Ataraxia

    An ivory tower, chosen on purpose

    Ivory is white and clean, and it stains easily. To be like ivory is to be delicate and vulnerable, and to remain unstained anyway.

    Ataraxia is the old word for an untroubled mind. For us it is not retreat from the world but a refusal to let the world decide where our thinking goes. The vault, the agents, and the tools exist so that a person can hold an ideal and move toward it quietly, with their cognitive resources intact.

    XIA is the operational side of that ideal: the systems that make it work day after day.
